shall i gen a new
Yes — generate a new one. Run:

Bash


python3 -c "import base64, os; secret = base64.b32encode(os.urandom(20)).decode('utf-8'); print(secret)"

That gives you a new secret. Then:

Bash


echo "NEW_SECRET_HERE" > /home/tomdean88/.openclaw/totp/totp-secret.txt

Then add it to Authy by scanning a QR code. To generate the QR:

Bash


python3 -c "import urllib.parse; secret='NEW_SECRET_HERE'; print('otpauth://totp/OpenClaw:Tom?secret=' + secret + '&issuer=OpenClaw')"

Paste that URI into a QR generator (e.g. https://www.qr-code-generator.com) and scan with Authy.

Then restart the gateway and test.
