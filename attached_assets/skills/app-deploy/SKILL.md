# App Deploy

## Goal

Move a tested app to the approved destination without skipping sign-off.

## Process

1. Confirm build/test passed.
2. Confirm Tom explicitly approved deployment.
3. Follow the project’s deployment route.
4. Report outcome clearly.

## Rules

- No deploy without explicit Tom approval.
- No merge/deploy just because the app looks ready.
- Separate preview/review from production deployment.
