# Runtime contract repair

**Repair mode:** source and documentation only. No live service, timer,
crontab, gateway, ledger, mailbox, or WhatsApp runtime was inspected or
mutated.

**Scope:** `attached_assets/install-forked-openclaw.sh`, the checked-in
expense watcher units and operating documentation, scheduling policy text, and
the WhatsApp recent-feed generator. Finance data, credentials, deployment, and
unrelated management/Google/invoice paths are out of scope.

## Fixed source contract

The following facts are now explicit and testable in this checkout:

1. **The central mirror-router is the intended ordered trigger.** The
   `pi-services/systemd-user/expense-intake-watcher.service` and `.timer` files
   remain in source control as a **legacy, opt-in fallback**. They are not
   evidence that the timer is active.
2. **The canonical installer does not install or enable the legacy watcher
   pair.** It also does not disable, remove, or migrate any unit that might
   already exist on a Pi. This prevents an installer run from creating a second
   five-minute trigger while retaining a checked-in rollback declaration.
3. **The retained service uses systemd's `%h` home specifier** rather than a
   user-specific `/home/...` path. Its source identity is therefore consistent
   with the installer's `$HOME/openclaw` model without asserting which checkout
   is live.
4. **The fallback cadence is unchanged at five minutes.** Keeping the
   declaration and its `OnBootSec`/`OnUnitActiveSec` values preserves
   recoverability; no live timer was disabled or enabled by this repair.
5. **The 06:xx/07:xx schedule inconsistency remains pending.** The scheduling
   documentation (`knowledge/pi-deployment.md` and `replit.md`) now labels the
   06:55 system-health and Monday 06:00 AI-briefing entries as pre-existing
   installer declarations, not approval evidence. They remain unchanged pending
   owner/runtime reconciliation and are not templates for new work. Existing
   cron expressions in the installer were not changed; no schedule decision was
   made by this repair.
6. **The WhatsApp recent feed uses the approved semantic 48-hour window.**
   Both the shell setting and the embedded renderer use 48 hours, and the
   generated `whatsapp-recent-window.json` sidecar records the same window.
   A missing or failed transcript now atomically replaces the sidecar with
   `coverage_status: incomplete` and null message counts; a readable empty
   transcript alone may report a verified zero. The generated feed likewise
   distinguishes unavailable coverage from a genuine no-message window.
   Retention limits remain fixed at the latest 8 messages per direct thread,
   500 group messages, and 1200 total messages, but any content dropped by
   those limits now marks the sidecar incomplete. Malformed or otherwise
   unrenderable records likewise prevent a complete status. When valid rows
   remain, `WHATSAPP_RECENT.md` prints explicit coverage warnings for source
   failures, parse loss, and retention truncation rather than presenting the
   retained rows as a complete or no-data result.
   The documentation explicitly rejects widening routine checks to 72 hours or
   falling back to the full log. The pinned `whatsapp-check` contract recorded
   by the operations audit is version
   `43abbcac-90a5-405d-8806-b7d85f5cc8b3` and specifies `WHATSAPP_RECENT.md`
   as a rolling 48-hour feed. The retained `.local` skill copy is audit
   context, not deployment proof.

## Recoverability and opt-in boundary

The legacy unit files are intentionally retained. No state, queue, receipt,
ledger, credential, or service artifact was deleted. An operator must first
identify the live owner and satisfy the duplicate-lifecycle, rollback, and
destination-readback gates in
[`runtime-ownership-audit.md`](runtime-ownership-audit.md) before considering
an opt-in or retirement change. In particular:

- do not enable the five-minute timer merely because the unit is checked in;
- do not disable an existing timer or central router based on this document;
- do not replace the central router with `watcher.py` until its source and
  `ExecStartPost` relationship are proven;
- preserve the rollback copy and canonical runtime state until the live
  identity and one controlled interval are recorded.

## Tests added and validation

`pi-services/systemd-user/test_runtime_contract.py` is a static/unit
configuration suite. It checks:

- explicit legacy/opt-in/not-installed markers on both expense units;
- portable `%h/openclaw` service paths and absence of the old hardcoded user;
- absence of installer commands that install, start, restart, or enable the
  legacy watcher pair;
- unchanged installer schedule expressions and explicit pending schedule
  inconsistency (without silently grandfathering approval);
- 48-hour WhatsApp shell/renderer configuration, atomic incomplete-source
  handling, missing/failed/empty runtime behavior, and offline fixtures for
  nine direct rows, mixed malformed/valid rows, the 500-row group cap, and
  the 1200-row total cap. These fixtures verify that dropped or lost content
  sets `coverage_complete: false` and that rendered Markdown retains a clear
  warning without making a false no-data claim;
- phone-first Google recovery instructions matching Calendar `--auth` on 8765
  and Gmail `--auth` on 8766 without token deletion guidance;
- presence of this fixed-source/live-unknown/recoverability record.

This is source/configuration validation only; it is not proof of live
